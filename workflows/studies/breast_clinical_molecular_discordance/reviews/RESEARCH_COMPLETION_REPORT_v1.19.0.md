# NAS-BRCA-002 Research Completion Report 1.19.0

Phase 0 is complete. Phase 1 remains in progress; Phases 2–6 have not started.
The study is not ready for final human review, and no scientific conclusion,
publication, or submission is authorized.

Research-use whole-transcriptome bulk RNA sequencing is selected as the
prospective primary-calibration platform family in planning only. The primary
unit remains paired aliquots of the same homogenized RNA with independent
library preparation and randomized sequencing placement. The exact chemistry,
instrument, vendor, specimen source, and budget remain unresolved and
unauthorized.

The next Phase 1 action is to freeze the intended specimen and RNA-quality range
and a performance-blind chemistry-selection gate. A degraded-RNA contingency
cannot inherit the high-quality-RNA reference bridge without separate
conformance. Technical-error inputs, a compatible final pair-count
reestimation, and reliability thresholds remain unresolved. Contact, quotes,
spending, procurement, specimens, controlled data, clinical use, publication,
and submission remain prohibited. Final human review remains preserved.
