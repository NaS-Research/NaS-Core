# NAS-BRCA-002 Research Completion Report 1.15.0

Phase 0 is complete. Phase 1 remains in progress; Phases 2–6 have not started.
The study is not ready for final human review, and no scientific conclusion,
publication, or submission is authorized.

The blinded pair-count reestimation gate is complete. The 13 independent public
pilot groups provide source-specific expression agreement, but not the locked
classifier, target-assay, attrition, clustering, or coverage inputs required for
the primary-calibration sample size. The gate therefore returned no final pair
count and made no proxy substitution. The previous 185-pair balanced result
remains hypothetical, and 30 excluded prospective pairs remain a planning
target only.

The next Phase 1 action is to select and freeze the intended assay and
preprocessing bridge, then specify evidence-backed QC, failure, and rerun rules.
A target-matched excluded pilot remains necessary before final pair-count and
threshold calibration. External contact, spending, specimens, controlled data,
outcomes, classifier execution, export, publication, and submission remain
prohibited. Final human review remains preserved.
