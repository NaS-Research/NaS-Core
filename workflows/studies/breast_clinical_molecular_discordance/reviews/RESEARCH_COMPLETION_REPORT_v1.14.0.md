# NAS-BRCA-002 Research Completion Report 1.14.0

Phase 0 is complete. Phase 1 remains in progress; Phases 2–6 have not started.
The study is not ready for final human review, and no scientific conclusion,
publication, or submission is authorized.

The prespecified source-isolated replicate pilots are complete. GSE60788's six
independent groups had median PAM50 Spearman correlation 0.985834 and median
RMSE 0.195806 on the unchanged supplied scale. GSE130397's seven independent
groups and 15 within-group pairs had median Spearman 0.983288 and median RMSE
0.361678 on `log2(CPM + 1)`. Both sources show high within-source rank agreement
and nonzero, source-dependent technical variation. Their absolute-error values
are not common-scale effect sizes.

The next Phase 1 action is blinded precision and pair-count reestimation using
the source-specific group estimates, followed by evidence-backed QC, failure,
and rerun criteria. Neither excluded source is eligible for primary calibration,
and neither can set a clinical reliability threshold. External contact,
spending, specimens, controlled data, outcomes, classifier execution, export,
publication, and submission remain prohibited. Final human review remains
preserved.
