## What “100%” means

The study reaches the requested endpoint only when Phases 0 through 5 are
complete, Phase 6 contains a complete immutable research release, and that
release is presented to the founder with status
`awaiting_final_human_review`.

Passing software tests, finishing literature review, producing a draft
manuscript, or generating interesting exploratory results cannot independently
set the study to 100%.

Publication and submission remain separate actions after final human review.
Neither is authorized by research completion.

external-validation leakage.

planning-only decision. That decision does not authorize contact, spending,
