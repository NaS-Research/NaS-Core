## What changed in 1.2.0

Standing founder autonomy now permits routine reversible internal scientific,
statistical, engineering, and project-management decisions without serial
approval packets. It retains hard stops for spending, credentials or paid
access, external contact, controlled data, PHI, specimens, destructive or
irreversible actions, clinical use, publication, submission, and material
changes to the study purpose or intended claim.

The internally frozen plan specifies:

- bulk RNA sequencing without vendor-stack selection;
- same-RNA independent libraries as the primary repeat architecture;
- a permanently excluded 30-pair feasibility pilot;
- marginal receptor, RNA-quality, blinded-margin, and processing-position quotas;
- one primary label-retention estimand;
- Holm-controlled confirmatory continuous endpoints;
- descriptive failure and abstention measures;
- exploratory gene-level FDR control; and
- an eight-criterion platform gate and price-free symbolic budget.

The plan is protocol evidence, not analytical-validity evidence. No data were
accessed, no source or threshold was selected, no final sample size was approved,
and no external action or execution is authorized.

## Current hard gate

Phase 1 closes only after platform compatibility, the locked reference and
preprocessing bridge, excluded-pilot nuisance estimates, blinded final size,
technical-error model, thresholds, abstention rule, failure criteria, numerical
tolerances, and multidisciplinary method reviews are complete without outcome or
external-validation leakage.

## Final-review boundary

The final human review remains preserved. Publication and submission remain
separate prohibited actions until the completed frozen release is reviewed and
the founder separately authorizes an external action.
