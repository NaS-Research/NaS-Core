# CHTN eligibility inquiry 1.0.0

**Subject:** Eligibility inquiry for an excluded breast-tumor RNA technical pilot

Hello CHTN Central Coordinator,

My name is Dalron J. Robertson, and I am the founder of NaS. I am writing to ask
whether CHTN may be an eligible source for a future, excluded, nonclinical
technical pilot. This is an information-only inquiry—not an application, tissue
request, quote acceptance, purchase, reservation, or collection request.

NaS is planning a research-use analytical pilot of a fixed PAM50 RNA-sequencing
workflow. The pilot would use 30 independent primary breast-tumor sources and
compare two independently prepared libraries from the same homogenized RNA.
Pilot specimens and results would remain excluded from primary calibration and
external validation and would not be used for diagnosis, prognosis, treatment,
or other clinical decision-making.

Could you please advise whether CHTN could potentially support the following?

1. Thirty unique primary breast-tumor sources as purified total RNA, or one
   standardized RNA extraction per source before the RNA is split for two
   independent library preparations.
2. Eligibility screening for 25–1000 ng of DNase-treated total RNA per library,
   RIN at least 8, finite quantity, purity measurements, and fragment analysis,
   without reserving or collecting specimens at this stage.
3. Enough RNA from the same homogenate for two independent library preparations,
   with a stable nonidentifying source key proving that each source is unique and
   supporting later nonoverlap checks.
4. Breast-tumor pathology and receptor-status information. We are not requesting
   direct identifiers, longitudinal outcomes, chart review, or PHI.
5. A high-level description of the application, human-subjects or use agreement,
   publication/acknowledgment, retention/destruction, shipping, biosafety, and
   institutional requirements that would apply only if NaS later chose to proceed.
6. Whether this request is plausible within CHTN's prospective collection model,
   and which division or coordinator would be appropriate for a later formal
   feasibility discussion.

Please do not initiate an application, tissue request, procurement, reservation,
or collection from this inquiry. No quote, purchase, specimen, controlled data,
PHI, or clinical service is requested. Any later application or transaction
would require a separate decision by NaS.
