## Draft A — Hurson et al. same-RNA PAM50 replicates

Proposed recipient: Melissa A. Troester, corresponding author identified in
PMCID `PMC10147733`  
Subject: Research inquiry regarding deidentified PAM50 technical-replicate data

Dear Dr. Troester,

I lead NaS, an independent precision-medicine research company. We are developing
a preregistered, research-only study of patient-independent PAM50 classification
reliability and abstention. Your 2023 report of 144 same-RNA technical-replicate
pairs is directly relevant to separating measurement repeatability from spatial
and biological heterogeneity.

Before requesting any data transfer, I would like to ask whether a deidentified
sample-level representation might be available under an appropriate agreement.
The minimum fields we would seek are:

- a stable, nonidentifying replicate-pair identifier;
- normalized values for the PAM50 genes for each technical replicate;
- intrinsic-subtype and continuous correlation or score outputs, if releasable;
- assay batch, QC, normalization, and replicate-generation metadata; and
- documentation of allowed research use, retention, publication, and derived
  artifact sharing.

We would not request direct identifiers, dates, clinical outcomes, treatment
data, geography, or a reidentification key. Any material would remain outside
Git, be registered under its actual access class, and would not be used until a
data-use and statistical review were complete. We would also be glad to discuss
an analysis performed within your controlled environment if export is not
permitted.

Would this type of deidentified technical-replicate resource be available, and
if so, what institutional process should we follow?
