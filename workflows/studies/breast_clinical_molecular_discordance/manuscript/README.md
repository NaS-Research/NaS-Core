# NAS-BRCA-002 Living Manuscript

This directory contains the evolving scientific paper for question `0.3.0`.
`WORKING_MANUSCRIPT.md` is the paper itself—not a project log, marketing document,
or final publication.

## Update rule

Update the manuscript after each material evidence appraisal, protocol decision,
analysis run, figure, sensitivity analysis, or review decision. Every update must:

1. preserve the distinction between prior literature and NaS-generated results;
2. cite an exact appraisal, analysis artifact, or external source;
3. label interpretation as interpretation;
4. retain null, contradictory, and failure-heavy evidence;
5. avoid novelty, clinical-utility, diagnostic, or treatment claims until authorized;
6. leave unsupported sections as explicit placeholders rather than filling gaps with prose;
7. update the evidence-to-text ledger inside the manuscript; and
8. record the date and reason in the manuscript revision log.

## Status vocabulary

- `placeholder`: structure exists; evidence or analysis is not yet available.
- `working`: supported content exists but the evidence phase is incomplete.
- `review-ready`: all cited artifacts are frozen and internally reviewed.
- `frozen`: content belongs to an immutable research release.

Only a frozen research release may become the website or journal-submission version.
