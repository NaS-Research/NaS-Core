# Prospective RNA-Quality and Chemistry Gate Report 1.0.0

`prospective_rna_quality_gate_receipt_v1.0.0.yaml` has SHA-256

It was frozen from revision `8ef5d4d` without study values, external contact,

The primary analytical-use range is purified total RNA from homogenized primary
breast-tumor material, limited to post-extraction analytical error. RNA must be
within the documented 25–1000 ng input range, have RIN at least 8, receive DNase
treatment, and have finite quantity, purity, and fragment-analysis measurements.
Paired aliquots of the same homogenized RNA must undergo independent library
preparation.

The selected chemistry family is stranded poly(A) whole-transcriptome mRNA
sequencing. Degraded or FFPE-derived RNA and repeated extraction are outside the
primary scope. They may enter only through a separate future amendment with a
new reference, transformation bridge, error model, and validation plan. Any
input failure occurs before scoring but remains in the attempted denominator.

This is a conservative planning boundary, not evidence that the assay is
reliable. Exact kit, normalized input mass, instrument, read layout, depth,
controls, specimen source, pilot size, and budget remain unresolved.
