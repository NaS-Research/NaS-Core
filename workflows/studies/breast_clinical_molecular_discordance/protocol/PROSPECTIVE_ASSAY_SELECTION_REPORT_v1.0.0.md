# Prospective Primary-Calibration Assay Selection Report 1.0.0

`prospective_assay_selection_receipt_v1.0.0.yaml` has SHA-256
`b4ba823e0b8eed6436e40bd8cf3fd5c6de94a4f5a619c87e8a3701dabb2302f2`.
It was frozen from implementation revision `2941126` without molecular,
validation, or outcome access and without external contact, procurement,
spending, specimen acquisition, or study execution.

The selected planning family is research-use whole-transcriptome bulk RNA
sequencing. The primary technical unit remains two aliquots of the same
homogenized RNA processed through independent library preparation, library QC,
randomized sequencing placement, and independent bioinformatic processing.
This choice is closest to the retrospective RNA-seq program, supports all 50
PAM50 genes, and preserves whole-transcriptome quality diagnostics.

This is deliberately not an exact product selection. Poly(A)-capture workflows
are designed for high-quality RNA, while degraded or FFPE material may require
total-RNA depletion or capture enrichment. Those alternatives change the
measurement process and cannot inherit the frozen reference or transformation
bridge without separate conformance. Targeted hybridization and amplicon assays
were not selected for the primary program because they constitute materially
different measurement families requiring distinct reference, calibration, and
validation programs.

Before any quote or experiment, NaS must freeze the intended specimen format,
RNA quantity and quality range, failure rules, exact chemistry-selection gate,
read layout, depth, platform bridge, and pilot design. Nothing in this receipt
authorizes clinical use or asserts analytical reliability.
