## Active rule

The evidence review now maintains:

1. an uncapped saturation inventory containing every founder-included record and
   its appraisal, restriction, or canonical duplicate/version link; and
2. a core synthesis of no more than 30 non-duplicate primary studies selected
   only after appraisal for directness, quality, unchanged external transport,
   and unique contribution.

All eligible evidence remains traceable even when it is not selected for concise
narrative synthesis. Selection cannot depend on result direction, statistical
significance, access convenience, or support for the NaS hypothesis.
