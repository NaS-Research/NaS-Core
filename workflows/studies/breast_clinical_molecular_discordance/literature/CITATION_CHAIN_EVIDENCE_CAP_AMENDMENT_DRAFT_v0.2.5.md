# Draft Evidence-Cap Amendment Triggered by Citation Pass 1

Proposed protocol version: `0.2.5`  
Current locked protocol: `0.2.4`  
Status: **Draft only—cannot govern research until founder approval**

## Trigger

Protocol `0.2.4` capped the final evidence set at 30 studies unless a versioned
amendment demonstrated that the stopping rule could not otherwise be assessed.
Citation pass 1 now has 32 founder-confirmed inclusions after complete screening of
4,495 candidates. Exact-identifier reconciliation found no match to the active
30-record inventory, three matches to locked prior appraisals, and 29 net-new
records. The cumulative provisionally eligible inventory therefore exceeds the
fixed cap before full-text eligibility and quality appraisal.

Excluding a directly eligible method merely because the current inventory already
contains 30 studies would violate the locked eligibility criteria and could create
selection bias. Automatically adding all 32 to the narrative synthesis would also
conflate title-and-abstract eligibility with full-text quality.

## Proposed amendment

Replace the fixed 30-study evidence-set cap with two separately governed layers:

1. **Saturation inventory:** no numerical cap. Every founder-included citation
   record must receive lawful-access accounting and full-text eligibility/quality
   appraisal or an explicit access restriction/duplicate disposition.
2. **Core synthesis set:** target no more than 30 non-duplicate primary studies,
   selected only after appraisal by directness to the reliability specification,
   methodological quality, unchanged external transport, and unique contribution.

All eligible studies remain in the evidence ledger even when they are not selected
for the concise core synthesis. Excluded-from-core records retain their appraisal,
contradictions, limitations, and citation-chain role. Core-set selection cannot be
based on direction, statistical significance, availability convenience, or support
for the NaS hypothesis.

## Required safeguards

- Do not activate this amendment before the combined citation screening receives
  exact founder confirmation.
- Retrieve and appraise every newly included record before core-set selection.
- Link preprint/published versions and overlapping cohorts to canonical studies.
- Preserve null, contradictory, failure-heavy, and access-restricted evidence.
- Version the evidence matrix and novelty memorandum after appraisal.
- Reset citation-pass zero-yield counting if any new eligible study is added.
- Maintain the prohibition on molecular and outcome access until the revised
  evidence gate, method lock, three founder review perspectives, and a new `go`
  decision are complete.

## Decision required after screening confirmation

The founder must separately approve, revise, hold, or reject protocol amendment
`0.2.5`. Confirmation of citation screening decisions does not automatically
approve this amendment.
